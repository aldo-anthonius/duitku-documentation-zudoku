Welcome to the Duitku Disbursement V2 API! 

Our newly upgraded Disbursement (Send Money) API enables you to automate outgoing payments directly from your application with unprecedented ease. Whether you are paying vendors, distributing employee payrolls, or processing customer refunds, our single integration connects you to thousands of banks, e-wallets, and retail channels across Indonesia.

## What's New in V2: Smart Routing

In previous versions, merchants had to manually determine the payment type (e.g., RTOL, LLG, RTGS, or BI-FAST) for every transaction. 

**With Disbursement V2, manual routing is a thing of the past.** Our intelligent core engine now automatically determines the most efficient, fastest, and cost-effective transfer route behind the scenes. You simply tell us **who** to send the money to and **how much**, and Duitku handles the rest automatically based on the recipient's bank, transaction amount, and real-time network availability.

## Supported Capabilities

With a single unified API, you can seamlessly disburse funds across multiple channels:

### 1. Interbank Transfers
Connect and send funds to over 140 banks across Indonesia.
* **Instant Processing:** Standard transfers are processed in real-time (24/7) using networks like BI-FAST or direct Host-to-Host (H2H) connections.
* **High-Value Payouts:** For exceptionally large corporate transactions that exceed real-time limits (e.g., above IDR 1,000,000,000), our system will automatically route the transaction through secure clearing networks (LLG/RTGS) during Bank Indonesia's business hours.

### 2. E-Wallet Top-Ups
Instantly disburse funds to major e-wallets in Indonesia. Simply use the recipient's registered mobile phone number as the destination account. Available 24/7.

### 3. Cash Out (Over-The-Counter)
Perfect for reaching the unbanked population. This feature allows your customers or partners to claim physical cash directly at our partnered physical counters.
* **Supported Channels:** POS Indonesia and Indomaret.
* **Best for:** Cash refunds, offline user payouts, and cash remittances.
